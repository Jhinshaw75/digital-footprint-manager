Master Digital Footprint Removal Guide

Phase 1: Check Your Exposure (Have I Been Pwned)

  1. Check Your Email
    Go to Have I Been Pwned and enter your email address to check for corporate data breaches.
  2. Review Leaked Data
    Identify what specific information (passwords, phone numbers, addresses) was exposed.
  3. Secure Accounts
    Update compromised passwords immediately, enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA), and never reuse passwords.
  4. Hide Your Email on HIBP
    Go to the HIBP Opt-Out Page, enter your email, verify via confirmation email, and choose your preferred privacy visibility setting.

Phase 2: Remove Yourself from Data Broker Sites

Note: Remember to ignore and close all commercial upsells (like Onerep), as manual removals on these sites are 100% free.

1. TruePeopleSearch

  1. Open the Opt-Out Portal
    Go to the TruePeopleSearch Opt-Out Portal[cite: 1].
  2. Submit Initial Request
    Enter your email address, complete the verification check box, and submit the form[cite: 1].
  3. Check Verification Email
    Open your email inbox, locate the verification message, and click the secure link[cite: 1].
  4. Match Your Record
    Fill out your complete profile information to match your exact record[cite: 1].
  5. Confirm Removal
    Submit the form to queue your removal, which completes within 3 days[cite: 1].

2. FastPeopleSearch

  1. Open the Opt-Out Portal
    Go to the FastPeopleSearch Opt-Out Portal.
  2. Submit Initial Request
    Fill out your relationship status, name, and email address, then submit the initial form.
  3. Check Verification Email
    Open your email inbox for the verification message and click the provided link.
  4. Complete Identity Match
    Fill out the identity match form with your exact personal details and address.
  5. Confirm Removal
    Submit the form to confirm your removal request.

3. Whitepages

  1. Find Your Profile Listing
    Search for your name on Whitepages.com, accept the initial cookie/terms popup, and open your specific profile's "View Details" page.
  2. Copy the Profile URL
    Copy the exact profile URL directly from your browser's address bar at the top of the screen.
  3. Submit to Suppression Portal
    Navigate to the Whitepages Suppression Requests Page and paste your profile URL.
  4. Complete Verification
    Confirm your details, select your reason for removal, and complete the automated phone verification call to finalize. (Repeat this process for each unique profile URL if you have multiple listings).

4. Spokeo

  1. Go to the Opt-Out Page
    Navigate to the Spokeo Opt-Out Page.
  2. Find and Copy Profile URL
    Open Spokeo's main search page in a separate tab, search for your name, bypass the age verification prompt, open your specific profile card, and copy the profile URL from your browser's address bar.
  3. Fill Out the Form
    Return to the Spokeo Opt-Out page, paste your profile URL, type your email address, complete the CAPTCHA box, and click Opt Out.
  4. Verify via Email
    Open the confirmation email from Spokeo in your inbox and click the secure link to finalize your removal.
