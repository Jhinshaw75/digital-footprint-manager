Your step-by-step interactive guide to online privacy, scam prevention, and digital safety.

Phase 1: Secure Your Accounts

Ensure your primary email and important accounts use strong, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication.

Phase 2: Remove Public Listings

Opt out of data broker sites that publish your personal contact details online.

Phase 3: Photo Privacy & Metadata Stripping

Learn how to strip hidden GPS data and metadata from photos before sharing them online.

Why this matters: Smartphones automatically embed hidden information (EXIF data) like exact GPS coordinates, timestamps, and device models into your pictures. Sharing these photos can accidentally reveal where you live or where you frequent.

  1. Open your Gallery or Google Photos app and select the picture you want to share.
  2. Swipe up on the photo or tap the three-dot menu to view details and check if location data is attached.
  3. When sharing directly, look for share options or use a metadata scrubbing tool if necessary.

Permanent Fix (Stop saving location for future photos): Open your Camera app settings, look for Save location or Location tags, and switch it OFF.

  1. Open the Photos app and select the image.
  2. Tap the Share button, then tap Options at the top of the screen.
  3. Toggle off Location before sending or sharing the image file.

Permanent Fix (Stop saving location for future photos): Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Camera, and select Never.

Phase 4: Email Masking & Aliases

Protect your main inbox by using masked email services when signing up for web services.
