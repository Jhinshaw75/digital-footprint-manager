How to Remove Location from Phone Photos:
• iPhone (iOS): Open the photo in Photos, swipe up or tap the "Info" icon, and tap "Adjust" to remove the location, or go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Camera and select "Never".
• Android: Open the photo in Gallery, tap the three dots or swipe up for details, tap "Edit" next to location data to remove it, or open your Camera app settings and toggle off "Save Location".
